Design & Architecture Notes

Technical brief prepared by Az. Consultant outlining the visual choices, factual calibration, accessibility safeguards, and edge delivery architecture behind this redesign proposal.

System Architecture Wiring

01 · SEMANTIC CORE

Zero Runtime HTML & CSS

Pure layered CSS tokens with zero client framework runtime, eliminating render blocking compilers and preserving instant paint across European and Asian networks.

02 · ZERO-MOTION EDITORIAL

Quiet Luxury & Instant Read

Removed CPU-intensive canvas particle waves in favor of instant Swiss typographic layout, sub-140ms hover transitions, and immediate readability for executive partners.

03 · COLOR & ACCESSIBILITY

System Aware Light & Dark

Native color scheme meta wiring with early inline FOUC prevention, cross tab sync, native modal dialogs, keyboard focus rings, and inert drawer traps.

04 · EDGE DELIVERY

Cloudflare Edge & OG Logo

Hardened response headers, dedicated custom 404 route, and verified 1200x630 plus 600x600 monogram assets for clean WhatsApp and LinkedIn previews.

Why This Direction Fits Katia's Brand

Her career spans Swiss digital asset banking at AMINA Bank (Zug) and senior talent acquisition at SwissChain. Instead of generic dark gradients or synthetic portraits, the visual language pairs Swiss typographic restraint with warm alabaster light surfaces and calibrated dark graphite panels.

Collaboration Readiness

Every section, text token, domain link, and contact endpoint can be wired directly to a custom domain and live mail relay on handoff. Prepared cleanly by Az. Consultant with full static portability.
