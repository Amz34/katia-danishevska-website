About

A decade of connecting the right people

I am a talent acquisition professional with around 10 years of experience supporting recruitment across financial services, technology, and digital assets.

Throughout my career, I have worked with different teams and business environments across Europe and international markets, including valuable professional experience in Switzerland. These experiences have shaped a broader understanding of talent markets, cross-cultural collaboration, and the challenges of hiring for growing businesses.

My focus is on connecting companies with the right people, from experienced specialists and managers to senior leaders across fintech, blockchain, and digital asset businesses.
